Marombando privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-24

In short

  • Your workouts stay on your phone.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Health Connect and Google Drive are optional and used only to store your own data.

Marombando is a workout log. It has no accounts of its own, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your phone

Your workouts, weights, custom exercises and settings are stored only on your phone, inside the app. Nobody else can see them. "Erase all data on this phone" in the app removes them.

Health Connect (optional)

If you allow it, the app:

  • writes each workout you finish (start time, end time, "strength training") to Health Connect, so your Google Health app and Fitbit can show it;
  • reads your heart rate for the time of each workout, to show the average and maximum in your history.

This data is used only to show it to you. It is never sent to the developer or any server by the app, and it is never used for advertising. You can remove the permissions at any time in Health Connect settings.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you sign in with Google, the app saves a backup file of your workout log in a private folder of your own Google Drive. Only Marombando can open that folder, and the app cannot see any other file in your Drive. The file goes directly from your phone to Google; the developer never receives it. You can delete the backup, and sign out, from the app, or remove access in your Google Account settings. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Backup files you create

"Save" and "Share" make a file that you control. What you do with it is up to you.

Not medical advice

Marombando is not a medical device and gives no medical advice. The Coach suggestions are general fitness ideas; if you have pain or a health condition, check with a health professional.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app and on the store page. Questions: bunstertengelmann@outlook.com
