Everything you need to train, nothing you don't

Built for lifters who want a fast log, not a social network.

Push / Pull / Legs log

Enter weight and reps for every exercise. Last session's numbers are carried over, so you always know what to beat.

Workout timer

Start and finish a workout, see how long it took, and see which day you did last and which is next.

Progress charts

Follow each lift over time, with your best, your latest and your total change, plus workouts per month.

Offline coach

Tell it your goal, experience, equipment and aches, in English, French or Portuguese. It suggests exercises you can add with a tap, and it works without internet.

Google Health sync

Optional. Finished workouts are added to Health Connect so Google Health and Fitbit show them, and the app shows your average heart rate.

Private Drive backup

Optional. Back up to a hidden folder in your own Google Drive and restore on a new phone. Nobody else can see it.

Your language, your units

Switch between English, Français and Português, and between kilograms and pounds, whenever you like from the Settings cog.

  • Every screen, message and exercise name is translated.
  • The offline coach understands all three languages.
  • Weights you already logged keep their unit and are converted when shown.

Your data stays yours

No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no account of our own.

✓ On your phone

Your workouts live inside the app on your device. We never receive them, and one tap erases them all.

✓ Only if you say yes

Health Connect and Google Drive are off until you turn them on, and only ask for what they need.

✓ You stay in control

Delete your Drive backup, sign out, or remove permissions at any time, from the app or your Google settings.
